I was nine, maybe, and it was summer, and the power went out in our building. The whole afternoon. It was so hot that we were all just sitting on the stairwell because it was the coolest place. And the old man from upstairs started telling this story about a fox that lived in a well, and he never finished it, he just got called inside. And I was so... I was almost angry about it. So that night I wrote the ending myself, in this exercise book with the grid lines, the kind you use for practicing characters. It was terrible. The fox just gets out of the well and goes home. But I remember the feeling of the pencil going faster than I could think. Like the story already knew where it was going and I was just running after it. I've been trying to get that feeling back pretty much ever since.
