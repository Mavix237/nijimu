It was a little silver digital camera, the kind that slid open. It was my uncle's, and he gave it to me because he'd bought a new one, so it wasn't even a gift exactly, it was more like a hand-me-down. I was six. And I took pictures of the worst things. Like the floor. The corner of the kitchen. My own feet on the bus. The screen on the back was so small and kind of green, so everything looked like it was underwater. My mom would scroll through and go, why is there no one in these? And I didn't have an answer then. I think I just liked that I could keep a corner of a room. That nobody else thought it was worth keeping, and I did. I still have the memory card somewhere. I'm almost scared to look at it, honestly, in case it's empty.
