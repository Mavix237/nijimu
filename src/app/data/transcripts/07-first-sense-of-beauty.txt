Once, we are talking about where our sense of beauty comes from, and it made me think back to learning piano as a child. Part of my earliest sense probably came through music. Melody taught me to feel line, rhythm, and all the subtle emotions in between, even before I learned to think visually. It made me sensitive to how things rise, pause, repeat, and build tension. In a way, music was one of the first things that taught me how to see.
